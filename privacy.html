Privacy and contact

What we ask for

The pilot form asks for your name, role, phone or WhatsApp number, and optionally your company, how many containers you handle in a month, the shipping lines and terminals you use, and your last extra charge. It stores these only if you tick the consent box.

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you about the pilot and to learn how clearing works in practice. We do not share them or sell them.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Delete your details

Ask us at any time and we will delete them. Write to oshomodelakure@gmail.com or message 08168460256.
