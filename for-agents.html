What you get

Two clocks

Days left on terminal storage and on line demurrage, counted from landing, with the date each starts charging.

Real rates, including step-ups

Rates come from the published tariffs. The total if the container stays shows what a delay costs, with the daily rate stepping up as it does on the terminal's invoice.

Missing steps

A list of the steps before release, with how many are left and which comes next.

An alert before charges start

A warning with time to act, also before a daily rate steps up.

Free time you agreed

Add the extra free days you agreed with the line or terminal, and the clock moves its dates.

Terminal statement field

Enter the terminal's charge statement and any waiver, so the record ends on the real figure.

A phone view

A single light screen that works on weak connections.

What the pilot asks of you

  • Tell us about your last container that went over its free days, and what caused it.
  • Tell us which free days and rates you actually get, since agreed free time can differ from the published tariff.
  • Try the clock on live containers and tell us what to change.

Not live yet

The pilot is for agents in Lagos. The first version relies on what you enter. It does not read data from shipping lines, terminals or government systems.
