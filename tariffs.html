What the tariffs say

The clock uses rates copied from tariffs that terminals and shipping lines have published. This page shows what it uses and where each figure comes from.

Terminal storage

Charged by the terminal for the space a container takes. Free for the first 5 days at APM, TICT, Lekki Freeport Terminal and PTML. It then steps up on day 6, day 9 and day 14.

Line demurrage

Charged by the shipping line for keeping its container. The Grimaldi tariff and the combined schedule both give 7 free days, then rates in bands.

Reefer, flat rack, open top, hazardous and out-of-gauge rates are in the clock. Pick them in the container type list.

Checked against a worked invoice

The PTML handbook includes a worked invoice for a 40 ft dry container landed on 1 May and cleared after 15 days. It shows storage of ₦549,600 for days 6 to 15 and demurrage of ₦140,600 for days 8 to 15.

The clock gives the same two figures for that container.

Things to know before relying on a figure

  • Rates exclude 7.5% VAT. PTML invoices also add a ₦100 stamp duty.
  • Reefer power and monitoring are separate daily charges that the clock does not add.
  • The combined schedule is dated November 2023 and does not name its carrier. Check that it is still current.
  • The Grimaldi and PTML import tariff reports say they take effect on 16 November 2026. The PTML handbook is dated 16 May 2026. The storage and demurrage rates match in both. Some other charges differ, such as late examination.
  • The combined schedule counts from 00:01 on the day the vessel arrives or berths, including weekends and public holidays.
  • Free time agreed on top of a tariff does not appear in these files. The clock has a box for it.
  • Terminals can change their tariffs, so check the date on the terminal's own page.
