Estimate. Rates are copied from the published tariffs listed on the tariffs page. They exclude reefer power and other terminal and line charges. Extra free days move the whole rate schedule back by that many days. The terminal's charge statement is the final figure. The dates and container shown when the page opens are samples.

Which container first?

When trucks, cash or paperwork limit what you can clear today, clear the container that costs most to hold one more day. Rates step up in bands, so the container with the most free days left is not always the safest to leave.
