How it works

Three steps for each container. In the first version the agent enters the details. Nothing connects to government or shipping line systems.

Step 1

Add a container

Enter the container number, terminal, shipping line, size, type and landing date. The clock fills in free days and rates from the published tariffs.

Steps before the container can leave

Each step is ticked as it is done. The list shows how many are still missing and which comes next.

  1. Shipping line release (delivery order)Shipping line
  2. Declaration lodged and duty assessedCustoms, B'Odogwu
  3. Duty paidBank
  4. NAFDAC or SON permitNational Single Window
  5. Physical examinationCustoms, at the terminal
  6. Terminal charges paidTerminal
  7. Exit note and gate releaseCustoms and terminal gate

The order and the steps can differ by cargo and by terminal. Agents can edit the list for each container.

What it does

  • Counts free days on two clocks from the landing date: terminal storage and line demurrage
  • Shows the date charges start and what each extra day costs, including the steps up in the daily rate
  • Counts working days left, since weekends use up free days but release work cannot happen on them
  • Ranks your containers by what one more day of holding costs, so you know which to clear first
  • Lists the steps still missing before release
  • Sends an alert before charges start and before a rate steps up
  • Keeps a dated log of each stage

What it does not do

  • It does not connect to the National Single Window, Customs, the port authority or shipping line systems.
  • It does not file declarations or pay anything for you.
  • It does not replace the terminal's charge statement, which is the final figure.
