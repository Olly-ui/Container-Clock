Questions

Is it live?

No. It is a concept in early design. The calculator uses the published tariffs, and the dates and containers shown when the page opens are samples.

Does it connect to the National Single Window or the port authority systems?

No. In the first version the agent enters the details. Connections depend on data access that has not been agreed.

Does it replace the National Single Window?

No. When we checked its public pages we found no free-day countdown or charge clock. Container Clock sits on top of the work the agent already does there and in other systems.

Where do free days and rates come from?

From the published tariff of the terminal and the shipping line you pick. We have APM Terminals Apapa, TICT, Lekki Freeport Terminal and PTML, and the Grimaldi schedule plus one combined demurrage schedule. For any other, enter your own free days and a daily rate. Free time you agreed on top can be added. The terminal's charge statement is the final figure.

Why two clocks?

The terminal charges storage for the space the container takes. The shipping line charges demurrage for keeping its container. They have different free days and different rates, so one can be running while the other is still free.

Why does the daily rate change?

Storage is charged in bands. At APM a 40 ft dry container pays ₦13,608 a day on days 6 to 8, ₦66,528 on days 9 to 13 and ₦90,720 from day 14. The clock shows the band you are in and warns you before the next one.

How are days counted?

Both clocks count the landing day as day 1 and include weekends and public holidays. This matches the PTML worked invoice, where a container landed on 1 May is charged storage from day 6 and demurrage from day 8. Confirm the start event with your terminal and line. If your agreement counts differently, tell us in the pilot.

What does the total include?

Storage and demurrage only, shown without VAT and with 7.5% VAT. It leaves out handling, delivery, examination, documentation, reefer power and other charges.

Which ports does it cover?

Lagos first. The pilot asks which shipping lines and terminals you use, so the first version fits the ones agents actually deal with.

Who pays for it?

This has not been decided. It is one of the questions the pilot is meant to answer.

How is my data handled?

The pilot form asks for your consent before storing your details. We use them only to contact you about the pilot and do not share them. See the privacy page.

Is this connected to NIMASA?

No. It is an independent concept prepared for NIMASA-BEAP National Innovation Hackathon, Track 05. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NIMASA.
