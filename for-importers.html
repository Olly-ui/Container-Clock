Later phases

For importers

The first version is built for the agent. The importer view comes after the agent clock has been tested.

What you will see

  • The days and charges on your containers
  • When your agent has finished each step
  • The date charges start, before they start

Later: find an agent

  • Post a job for a container or an export
  • Licensed agents respond
  • You and the agent share the same clock once the job starts

What we still need to learn

We have not yet interviewed importers. Before building an importer view we need to learn who carries the extra charges, how free days are agreed with the shipping line, and whether a clock alone is worth paying for.

Candidates to test: document checklists for each cargo type, a shared record of costs for each job, and reminders to importers.

Tell us how you work today

If you import or export through Lagos, tell us about the last container that went over its free days.
